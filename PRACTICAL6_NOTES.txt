Practical 5 extended for Practical 6.

Added: Src/ADC/{adc.h,adc.c}, Src/Ultrasonic/{ultrasonic.h,ultrasonic.c}.
Updated: Src/main.c (final Part F five-state FSM), CMakeLists.txt.
Kept unchanged: original UART and Kobuki drivers.

Hardware: MB1043 pin 6 -> 3V3, pin 7 -> GND, pin 3 -> PA0/A0.
Sensor and robot grounds must be common.

FIRST TEST: place robot on a block with the wheels off the table.
Build and hardware testing are still required on your STM32 toolchain.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS inherited from Practical 5/manual:
- UART_Rx / Kobuki_Rx block while waiting for data; stop button cannot be checked during that wait.
- The manual's AVOID delay is busy-wait, with no button polling.
- Kobuki_Read assumes a fixed received bytestream layout. Confirm feedback and button mappings on hardware.
- The manual FSM does not require a wheeldrop condition; consider one for your final autonomous project.
